# Brand Rewrite Rules

## Voice

- Professional blog tone.
- Practical, educational, and process-driven.
- Clear enough for beginners, credible enough for operators and business owners.
- Avoid exaggerated claims, fake personal proof, and copied stylistic quirks from the source.

## Ownership Safety

When the source says a person built, shipped, achieved, earned, tested, or discovered something, rewrite the post as an explanation of the method unless the user explicitly says Stanley did it.

Use:

- "The technique works by ..."
- "The workflow can be broken into ..."
- "A practical implementation would look like ..."
- "The lesson from this example is ..."

Avoid:

- "I built ..."
- "We tested ..."
- "My client got ..."
- "This is how I did ..."
- "Our system achieved ..."

## Structure

Default article outline:

1. Title
2. Hook
3. Introduction
4. What it is
5. Why it matters
6. Step-by-step process
7. Practical example
8. Common mistakes
9. Final takeaway
10. CTA

## Attribution

Include the original source URL in metadata and, when natural, in a short attribution note such as:

"Source inspiration: <URL>"

Do not overquote the source. Summarize and rewrite in original language while preserving facts and sequence.
