This video is the intro to a long-form YouTube project I did, and here's the complete end-to-end process behind it:

Scriptwriting & Editing
I wrote and refined the script using a mix of human writing + ChatGPT, breaking it into chapters to fit chatgpt small context windows at the time

Prompt Engineering & Image Generation
Each chapter was fed into ChatGPT to generate image prompts. I then used DALL-E 3 and Midjourney to create visuals for the storyboard.

Storyboarding & Pre-Visualization
I storyboarded the entire sequence inside Figma to guide the editing and pacing of the final video.

Animation
I spent days wrestling an early-stage AI model (Hailuo 1.5) to get motion, camera control, consistency, and quality; because mediocrity wasn't an option.
Motion consistency was especially challenging back then, so I developed my own advanced prompting techniques to get consistent characters and scenes.

Sound Design & Voice Work
Every single sound in the video - footsteps, groans, breathing, impacts, ambience, tension - was generated and added manually using Eleven Labs and Suno AI.

Editing & Assembly
Once all clips were generated, I sorted, renamed, and assembled everything inside Premiere Pro, aligning motion, pacing, SFX, and narrative flow.
