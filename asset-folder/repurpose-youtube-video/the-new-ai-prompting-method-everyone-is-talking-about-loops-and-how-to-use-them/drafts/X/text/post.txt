Most people are still asking AI for answers.

The better move is building loops.

A loop is simple:

discover -> plan -> execute -> verify -> improve

That is how AI stops being a one-off chatbot and starts becoming a repeatable workflow.

The important part:

Do not remove the human.

Move the human to the judgment points.

Optional thread:

1. Prompting is asking once.
Looping is designing a workflow the AI can repeat.

2. Good loops have stages:
discovery, planning, execution, verification, iteration.

3. Agents are useful when each one has a clear job.
Research agent. Builder agent. Marketing agent. QA agent.

4. The orchestrator is what keeps the system moving.
It decides what runs, when it runs, and what needs review.

5. The win is not "AI does everything."
The win is that AI handles repeatable work while humans protect quality.
