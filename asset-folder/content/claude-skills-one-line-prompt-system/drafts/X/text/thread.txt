1/ AI saves time when it becomes a workflow, not another tab. Claude Skills turn repeated long instructions into reusable one-line workflows.

2/ The prompt is not the asset. The repeatable process is. People keep retyping the same instructions instead of saving the process.

3/ Do not ask AI to guess your workflow. Give it the source, the rules, and the definition of done.

4/ The safest automation has a human review gate before anything reaches a client, customer, or audience.

5/ If a task happens every week, has clear inputs, and ends in a predictable output, it is a good AI workflow candidate.

6/ Move the recurring rules, voice, structure, and examples into a reusable skill. The win is not magic. It is repeatable operating leverage.
