Slide 1:
Stop saving prompts.
Start saving workflows.

Slide 2:
A prompt is one instruction.
A Skill is a reusable process.

Slide 3:
If the task repeats, the instructions should not live in your memory.
They should live in a system.

Slide 4:
A useful Skill needs clear input, role, rules, voice, output, QA, and approval.

Slide 5:
Generic Skills feel generic because they do not know your workflow.

Slide 6:
For content, the Skill should know your audience, offer, proof rules, tone, claims to avoid, and output folder.

Slide 7:
For business automation, the Skill should know the trigger, source data, decisions, handoff, and stop condition.

Slide 8:
The slash command is not the shortcut.
The reusable process behind it is.
