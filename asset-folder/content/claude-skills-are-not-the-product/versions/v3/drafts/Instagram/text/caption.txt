Claude Skills are not magic.

They are packaged workflows.

The real question is not:
"Which Skill should I download?"

The better question is:
"Which repeated task in my business should I turn into a reusable system?"

A useful Skill needs:

1. Clear input
2. Clear role
3. Clear rules
4. Clear voice
5. Clear output
6. Clear QA
7. Clear approval

That is how you move from random prompting to reliable AI work.

The prompt is not the asset.

The process is.
