1/ AI saves time when it becomes a workflow, not another tab. A useful AI stack is a chain of tools, skills, plugins, MCPs, QA, and publishing surfaces.

2/ The prompt is not the asset. The repeatable process is. Buying isolated AI tools does not create an operating system.

3/ Do not ask AI to guess your workflow. Give it the source, the rules, and the definition of done.

4/ The safest automation has a human review gate before anything reaches a client, customer, or audience.

5/ If a task happens every week, has clear inputs, and ends in a predictable output, it is a good AI workflow candidate.

6/ Design the stack as connected layers: research, browser, data, drafting, QA, image/video, approval, and publishing. The win is not magic. It is repeatable operating leverage.
