
I replaced one creator bottleneck with a content tool stack.

The source proof is 350k+ across socials, but the useful part is the org chart behind it.

The team:

Head of Operations: Hermes
Scores ideas and picks the winner.

Head of Research: Reddit, X, and Instagram
Finds what people actually want to read.

Head of Content: Claude
Turns rough ideas into posts and scripts.

Head of Design: Claude Code
Builds branded graphics in HTML.

Head of Illustration: GPT Image 2
Creates visuals on demand.

Head of Post-Production: HyperFrames
Cuts reels, captions, and overlays.

Head of Cinematography: Higgsfield
Generates the B-roll that was never shot.

Head of Distribution: Buffer
Queues and ships posts on schedule.

Head of Community: ManyChat
Handles comments, DMs, and guide delivery.

Head of Analytics: Apify
Pulls the numbers on what landed.

Head of Memory: Notion
Stores ideas, assets, numbers, and calendars.

The human still keeps the final yes.
