
I replaced one creator bottleneck with a content tool org chart.

The exact team behind 350k+ across socials:

For years, every post needed one person to think, research, write, design, edit, publish, reply, track, and remember everything.

That does not scale.

So the work became a team:

Head of Operations: Hermes
Scores every idea and picks the winner before production starts.

Head of Research: Reddit, X, and Instagram
Finds what people actually care about before the content gets made.

Head of Content: Claude
Turns rough ideas into posts and scripts in the right voice.

Head of Design: Claude Code
Builds branded graphics and infographics in HTML.

Head of Illustration: GPT Image 2
Creates visuals on demand when the post needs an image.

Head of Post-Production: HyperFrames
Cuts and renders reels, captions, and overlays.

Head of Cinematography: Higgsfield
Creates the B-roll footage that was never filmed.

Head of Distribution: Buffer
Queues and ships the content on schedule.

Head of Community: ManyChat
Handles comments, DMs, and guide delivery.

Head of Analytics: Apify
Pulls the numbers and shows what actually landed.

Head of Memory: Notion
Stores ideas, assets, calendars, metrics, and the messy history.

The useful part is not replacing the human.

The useful part is removing the bottleneck.

The human still keeps the last yes.
