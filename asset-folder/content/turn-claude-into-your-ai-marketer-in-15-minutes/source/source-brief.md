# Source Brief

## Core Idea

The video demonstrates a Claude-centered agentic marketing workflow that turns
one chat into a content factory: trend research, planning, creative production,
distribution, and optimization.

## Automation Insight

The useful shift is not only faster ad generation. It is workflow consolidation.
Research, planning, production, ad launch, performance reading, and creative
iteration can happen inside one agentic loop instead of across disconnected
tools, logins, and handoffs.

## Proof Points

- 14 minute 59 second LinkedIn video.
- 15,000 credits and around 3 hours of compute in the demo.
- Compared by the source post with $100k+ and two to four weeks from a
  traditional agency.
- Workflow includes trend crawling, calendars, scripts, 100 ad renders across
  five formats, Meta Ads via MCP, performance reading, scaling winners, and
  matching static creatives.

## Audience Angle

- LinkedIn: operators and agencies thinking about their 2026 AI marketing stack.
- X: concise point about agentic marketing being a full loop, not a prompt.
- Instagram: carousel teaching the five-layer agentic marketing stack.
- Facebook: beginner-friendly course angle around learning to build AI marketing
  workflows.

## Safety Notes

Do not imply Stanley personally ran the demo. Attribute proof as "the demo" or
"the source walkthrough." Do not promise guaranteed revenue. Tie speed claims to
workflow throughput, production capacity, and reduced coordination overhead.
