Slide 2 - The old stack
Research lived in one tool.
Planning lived in another.
Creative production lived somewhere else.

Slide 3 - The new loop
The walkthrough shows one agentic workflow:
research -> planning -> production -> distribution -> optimization.

Slide 4 - Research
The agent crawls TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube for live trends.
The goal is not random content.
The goal is better signal.

Slide 5 - Planning
Those signals become a content calendar with hooks and scripts.
This is where strategy enters the workflow.

Slide 6 - Production
The demo renders 100 ads across five formats.
That turns one campaign idea into a real testing batch.

Slide 7 - Distribution
Claude connects to Meta Ads through MCP.
Now the workflow can move from asset creation to campaign launch.

Slide 8 - Optimization
The loop reads performance, scales winners, and ships matching static creatives.
That is the difference between an AI tool and an AI marketing system.
