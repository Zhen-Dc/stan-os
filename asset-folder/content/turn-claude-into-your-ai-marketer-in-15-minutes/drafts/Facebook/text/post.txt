Most people are still learning AI as "what prompt should I type?"

This walkthrough shows the bigger skill:

How to build an AI marketing workflow.

The video breaks the process into five layers:

1. Research
Find live trends from platforms like TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube.

2. Planning
Turn those trends into a calendar, hooks, and scripts.

3. Production
Create many ad variations across different formats.

4. Distribution
Connect the workflow to an ad platform through MCP.

5. Optimization
Read performance, scale what works, and create matching static creatives.

That is why this matters for anyone learning AI skills.

The future is not just knowing how to generate one image or one script.

It is knowing how to design the full workflow from idea to launch to feedback.

If you can learn that, you can help businesses move faster without needing a
huge team or weeks of manual handoffs.
