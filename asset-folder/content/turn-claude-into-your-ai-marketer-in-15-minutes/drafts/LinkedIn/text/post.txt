Stop treating AI marketing like a prompt.

The real shift is the loop.

This 15-minute walkthrough shows Claude acting more like an AI marketing
operator than a writing assistant:

1. Crawl TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube for live trends.
2. Turn those signals into a content calendar with hooks and scripts.
3. Render 100 ads across five formats.
4. Connect to Meta Ads through MCP.
5. Launch campaigns, read performance, scale winners, and ship matching static
   creatives.

That is the useful part.

Not "make me an ad."

It is research -> planning -> production -> distribution -> optimization inside
one workflow.

The source demo says the full run used 15,000 credits and around 3 hours of
compute. It compares that with a traditional agency path that could cost $100k+
and take two to four weeks.

The lesson for founders, agencies, and operators:

Your 2026 marketing stack should not just generate assets.

It should connect the decisions before and after the asset:

- What trend is worth reacting to?
- What hook should we test?
- Which format should we render?
- Where should it launch?
- What signal decides the next creative?

That is where AI automation starts becoming an operating system instead of
another content tool.

If you want to build this safely, map the five layers first:

Research. Planning. Production. Distribution. Optimization.

Then decide where the human approval points belong.
