You don't have to spend hours scripting and editing video ads manually anymore.
I just built a Claude AI agent that handles the entire process for you.

It reports what worked, documents what failed, and creates new variations based only on the winning ads.
Then it repeats the entire process.

So instead of hiring separate researchers, scriptwriters, content creators, and media buyers, you have one AI agent creating and improving your video ads from start to finish without breaking the bank.
