Most brands do not have a video ad problem.

Research sits in one place.
Scripts sit in another.
Visuals sit in another.

Publishing is separate.
Performance data rarely changes the next creative fast enough.

The Claude + Higgsfield AI agent fixes the handoffs.

1. Research competitor ads and active creatives.
2. Break winning ads down scene by scene.
3. Write an original script in the brand voice.
4. Create image prompts, scene prompts, and motion prompts.
5. Generate and animate the scenes.
6. Edit the ad with captions, transitions, pacing, and sound.
7. Send it for approval before publishing.
8. Collect performance data.
