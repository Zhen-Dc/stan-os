1/ Most businesses do not have a video ad problem. They have a broken handoff problem between research, script, assets, editing, publishing, and performance data.

2/ An AI ad agent should not only create one video. It should learn from the ad after it runs.

3/ Do not copy competitor ads. Extract the hook, angle, pacing, visual pattern, and CTA. Then build original creative for your brand.

4/ The approval gate matters. Let the agent create and edit fast, but keep the brand decision human before publishing.

5/ A winning ad system needs a loop: research, create, edit, approve, publish, measure, vary, repeat.

6/ The real asset is not the first generated video. It is the system that documents what worked and makes the next variation smarter.
