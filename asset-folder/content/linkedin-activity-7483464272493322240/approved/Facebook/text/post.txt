A lot of people think AI for marketing means asking ChatGPT to "write a better ad."

That is the weak version.

The stronger version is building a workflow around a winning creative.

For example:

You give Claude the ad that worked.
You give it the comments.
You give it the performance notes.

Then it helps you score the creative, understand the angle, mine customer language, create hooks, build UGC scripts, and prepare briefs for the next test batch.

That is useful because marketing teams do not just need more copy.

They need a faster way to turn what already worked into the next set of testable creatives.

If you are learning AI skills, this is the direction to pay attention to:

Do not only learn prompts.
Learn how to turn a repeated business task into a repeatable AI workflow.
