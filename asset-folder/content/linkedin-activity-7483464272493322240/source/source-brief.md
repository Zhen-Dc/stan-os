# Source Brief

## Core Idea

Claude skills can turn one winning ad into a repeatable creative strategy pipeline.

## Source Mechanics

- Use slash-command skills.
- Feed Claude an existing creative, comments, and performance numbers.
- Return strategy artifacts such as rubrics, hooks, scripts, briefs, concepts, and channel-specific captions.
- Research skills: score the ad, tear down the angle, map awareness, mine comments, spot fatigue.
- Production skills: generate hooks, write UGC scripts, build briefs, generate variations, and adapt on-screen/sound creative for Meta and TikTok.

## Stanley B2B Angle

Target audience: App companies, SaaS teams, agencies, and marketing teams in the USA, UK, Canada, and similar markets.
Business function: UGC/ad creative production and creative testing.
Manual bottleneck: A winning ad gets found, but the team still has to manually score it, mine comments, write hooks, create briefs, and adapt it for each channel.
Automation angle: Use Claude skills as repeatable creative operators that convert old winners, comments, and numbers into new hooks, scripts, briefs, and test concepts.
Business outcome: Faster creative testing, more campaign angles, and less manual creative strategy work.

## Safety Notes

- Do not say Stanley built the source creator's skills.
- Do not invent campaign performance, revenue, CTR, CPM, or client names.
- Use Stanley-owned proof separately only when relevant. The safest angle here is workflow automation for marketing teams.
