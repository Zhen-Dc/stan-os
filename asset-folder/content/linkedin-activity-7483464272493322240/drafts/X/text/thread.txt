A winning ad should not sit in a folder.

It should become the input for the next test batch.

Use Claude to:

1. Score the ad
2. Tear down the angle
3. Mine the comments
4. Map the buyer stage
5. Generate hooks and scripts
6. Build the brief
7. Adapt the creative for the channel

Most teams use AI at the wrong step.

They ask it to invent new ads from nothing.

Better move:

Give it the creative that already worked.
Give it the comments.
Give it the numbers.
Then ask it to extract what is reusable.

That is how one ad becomes a creative system.
