
Writing emails from scratch is not the real problem.

Writing them without context is.

The stronger workflow is simple:

1. Capture meeting context.
Use Granola or meeting notes so the actual call does not disappear after Zoom, Teams, or Meet ends.

2. Connect the inbox and the notes.
Claude should see the Gmail thread and the meeting context together.

3. Point Claude to the right place.
Name the sender, topic, timeframe, and reply goal.

Prompt shell:

Pull [sender]'s email about [topic].
Search meeting notes for [topic] in [timeframe].
Draft a short warm reply that [confirms / answers / declines].

4. Save as draft.
Let Claude prepare the Gmail draft.
You review it before sending.

5. Start read-only.
Do not give write access on day one.
Watch how the system behaves first.

The win is not "AI wrote an email."

The win is that the reply uses the conversation that already happened.
