
If Claude writes emails without meeting context, it is guessing.

A better AI email workflow connects:

- the Gmail thread
- the meeting transcript or notes
- the sender
- the topic
- the timeframe
- the reply goal

Then Claude can draft from the conversation that actually happened.

The safest version:

1. Connect Gmail and meeting notes.
2. Start with read-only access.
3. Ask Claude to draft a short reply.
4. Save it as a Gmail draft.
5. Review before sending.

The point is not removing judgment.

The point is removing copy-paste and blank-page work while keeping context and review.
