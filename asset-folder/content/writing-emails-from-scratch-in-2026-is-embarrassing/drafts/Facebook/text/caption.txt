The useful lesson here is the workflow behind the tool:

1. People use AI to draft replies from the inbox thread alone, so the email misses what was actually said in meetings.
2. Capture calls in Granola, connect Gmail plus Granola in Claude desktop, point Claude to the sender/topic/timeframe/reply goal, and save the reply as a draft for review.
3. The public LinkedIn preview claims the workflow takes 2 minutes, includes a copy-paste prompt shell, and warns users to start with read-only access before granting write access.

That is how AI becomes useful in real work.

Not as a random prompt.
As a repeatable system with context, output, review, and handoff.
