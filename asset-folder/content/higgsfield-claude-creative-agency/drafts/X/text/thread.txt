1/ AI saves time when it becomes a workflow, not another tab. The future of creative work is not one better prompt. It is connected tools with a review gate.

2/ The prompt is not the asset. The repeatable process is. Claude plus a creative tool becomes useful when it can research, generate, track, and repeat.

3/ Do not ask AI to guess your workflow. Give it the source, the rules, and the definition of done.

4/ The safest automation has a human review gate before anything reaches a client, customer, or audience.

5/ If a task happens every week, has clear inputs, and ends in a predictable output, it is a good AI workflow candidate.

6/ If your creative workflow lives across five tabs, AI should coordinate the handoff. The win is not magic. It is repeatable operating leverage.
