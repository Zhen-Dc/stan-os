
If you are learning AI, start here:

Do not chase every new tool.

Study how one useful workflow is built.

This source shows a practical pattern:

- Creative teams lose time moving between idea boards, prompts, asset generation, research docs, spreadsheets, and manual follow-up.
- Connect Claude to Higgsfield through MCP or CLI, then use Claude Code, research docs, trackers, creative variations, skills, and scheduled routines.
- The walkthrough shows brand creation, marketing studio, image-to-ad generation, research documents, a Google Sheet tracker, variations, reverse-engineered skills, and routines.

That is the skill worth learning. You are not just learning how to prompt. You are learning how to turn a repeated task into a system.

Start with one task you already do every week. Write the steps. Let AI help with the draft. Then review the result before using it.

That is how AI becomes useful for work, content, and business.
