1/ AI saves time when it becomes a workflow, not another tab. A faceless YouTube workflow can be broken into source collection, scripting, asset creation, editing, QA, and publishing handoff.

2/ The prompt is not the asset. The repeatable process is. Content systems fail when every step is manual and nobody owns the handoff.

3/ Do not ask AI to guess your workflow. Give it the source, the rules, and the definition of done.

4/ The safest automation has a human review gate before anything reaches a client, customer, or audience.

5/ If a task happens every week, has clear inputs, and ends in a predictable output, it is a good AI workflow candidate.

6/ Break the channel into repeatable stages with source intake, generation, review, and publishing checkpoints. The win is not magic. It is repeatable operating leverage.
