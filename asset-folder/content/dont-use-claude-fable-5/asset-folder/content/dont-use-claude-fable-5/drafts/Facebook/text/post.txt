Claude Fable 5 is not the model I would use for every small task.

That is not how I would teach AI workflows.

The strongest model is usually the most expensive model.

So you should not use it for every small task.

Think of it like hiring a senior expert.

You do not bring the senior expert in to format documents all day.
You bring them in to understand the problem, ask better questions, build the plan, and set the rules.

Then a cheaper assistant can execute.

That is how beginners should learn AI:

Use the strongest model for:
- strategy
- research direction
- complex thinking
- workflow design
- decision rules

Use cheaper models for:
- summaries
- formatting
- simple drafts
- rewriting
- filling templates

The real skill is knowing when to switch.

That is what separates someone who "uses AI" from someone who can build an AI workflow.
