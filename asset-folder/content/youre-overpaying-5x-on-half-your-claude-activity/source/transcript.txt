You're overpaying 5x on half your Claude prompts.
Pick wrong and you burn your limits 2x faster:

Sonnet 5: 3x cost, 1M context. Best value of the four. Spends your limits the slowest. The hardest 10% still needs a bigger model. Use it for everyday drafting, rewrites, turning notes into a plan.

Opus 5: 5x cost, 1M context. Anthropic says start here if you're unsure. Slower, and it burns your limits much faster. Use it for strategy, contracts, multi-step analysis, anything where being wrong is expensive.

Fable 5: 10x cost, 1M context. Plans, builds and tests its own work. Twice the price of Opus for the same quality. Use it for long agentic builds you leave running.

Every model except Haiku has an effort dial: Low, Medium, High, Extra, Max. High is the default and the sweet spot. Medium is the cost step-down. Low is for bulk, classification and quick lookups. Extra is for long agentic runs over 30 minutes. Max is the trap because Anthropic describes it as significant cost for relatively small quality gains.

The post says the cheapest real upgrade is the dial, not the model: raising it costs nothing. It gives Claude Code commands: /model opus and /model sonnet, and advises starting with Opus 5 on High, then moving the dial before moving the model.
