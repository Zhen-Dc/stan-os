1/ Claude limits disappear faster when the wrong model is doing the wrong job.

2/ Haiku for cheap bulk. Sonnet for daily work. Opus for expensive mistakes. Fable for long agentic builds.

3/ The cheapest Claude upgrade is often the effort dial, not the model.

4/ High is the sweet spot. Low is for bulk. Extra is for long agentic runs. Max is the trap unless the gain is worth it.

5/ Do not spend 5x model capacity on a task that only needed a summary, reformat, or quick lookup.

6/ Move the dial before you move the model.
