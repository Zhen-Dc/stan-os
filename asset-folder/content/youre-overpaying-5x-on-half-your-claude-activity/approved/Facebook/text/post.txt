
Claude gets expensive when every job uses the same model.

That is the real lesson from this post.

If the task is simple, do not spend premium limits on it.

Use the ladder:

1. Haiku for cheap bulk work.
2. Sonnet for everyday drafting and planning.
3. Opus for work where mistakes are expensive.
4. Fable for long agentic builds that need planning, tools, and testing.

Then check the effort dial:

Low for quick bulk.
Medium for a cheaper step-down.
High as the sweet spot.
Extra for long agentic runs.
Max only when the gain is worth the cost.

This is how you stop burning Claude limits on work that never needed the bigger model.
