Claude gets expensive when every task uses the same model.

Use the model ladder:

1. Haiku for cheap bulk work.
2. Sonnet for everyday drafting and planning.
3. Opus when mistakes are expensive.
4. Fable for long agentic builds.

Then check the effort dial:

Low for bulk.
Medium for step-down.
High as the sweet spot.
Extra for long runs.
Max only when the gain is worth the cost.

The simple check:
if the task is low-risk, do not spend premium capacity on it.

Checkable detail:
Haiku is the 1x lane, Sonnet is 3x, Opus is 5x, and Fable is 10x.
