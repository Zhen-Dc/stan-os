## Business Job

This post should help marketing directors, heads of growth, creative
strategists, and agency leads pressure-test a campaign brief before budget goes
live by using five role-specific AI reviewers and one final decision memo.

## Department-Task Promise

- Department: marketing, growth, or creative strategy.
- Responsible buyer: marketing director, head of growth, creative strategy
  lead, or agency owner.
- Repeated task: review a campaign brief, positioning angle, offer, or creative
  direction before launch.
- Current friction: stakeholder review can repeat the same assumptions, while a
  single agreeable AI answer creates false confidence rather than useful
  challenge.
- Available input: campaign brief, proposed angle, constraints, customer
  evidence, and available performance data.
- Mechanism: five role-specific advisors independently test downside,
  assumptions, upside, outside perspective, and execution feasibility; they
  peer-review one another; a chairman synthesizes the disagreements.
- Usable output: ranked risks, assumptions to verify, unresolved disagreement,
  a go/rework/stop recommendation, and the next action.
- Faster/easier promise: replace an unstructured AI conversation with one
  repeatable pre-launch review workflow. The source provides no verified time
  reduction, so do not claim a number of hours saved.
- Guardrail: the marketing owner remains responsible for the decision and must
  verify evidence. Five agents can still share the same blind spot.
