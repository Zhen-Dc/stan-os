## Repeated Requirements Across The Market

### Core tools

- n8n, Make, Zapier, Workato, Power Automate.
- Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, OpenAI/Anthropic APIs.
- Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, GitHub Copilot.
- Airtable, Notion, Google Sheets, Google Workspace, Slack.
- HubSpot, Salesforce, CRMs, ATS/HRIS, Jira, Confluence.
- Microsoft Copilot Studio, Power Platform, Microsoft 365, Microsoft Graph.
- LangChain, LlamaIndex, RAG, vector databases.
- MCP servers, function calling, tool calling, webhooks, APIs.

### Technical requirements

- APIs, REST, JSON, OAuth/authentication, webhooks.
- Python, JavaScript, SQL; sometimes Java/Go.
- Workflow triggers, routers, filters, conditional logic, retries, error handling.
- Prompt engineering, structured outputs, function calling, tool use.
- RAG, embeddings, semantic search, knowledge-base grounding.
- Agent orchestration, multi-agent routing, memory/context management.
- Logging, monitoring, evaluation, QA, test cases, drift detection.
- Documentation, workflow diagrams, playbooks, training guides.
- Security, governance, access control, privacy, auditability, responsible AI.

### Business requirements

- Map business processes before building.
- Identify repetitive manual work and bottlenecks.
- Turn vague business needs into technical specs.
- Build working systems, not just recommendations.
- Prove impact using hours saved, cost saved, success rate, error rate, adoption, revenue, or pipeline generated.
- Train non-technical teams and write clear handoff documentation.
- Keep improving deployed automations after launch.

## Most Common Tasks Companies Want Automated

- Lead follow-up, CRM updates, outbound sequencing, enrichment, and pipeline generation.
- Customer support triage, routing, recurring-question reduction, and AI customer service.
- Scheduling, intake, validation, approvals, reminders, and escalation paths.
- Reporting dashboards, analytics, KPI tracking, and automated summaries.
- Documentation generation, knowledge-base search, workflow diagrams, and playbooks.
- Content production, repurposing, marketing workflows, email analysis, and social/ad platform data flows.
- Data extraction, classification, tagging, metadata management, OCR/document processing.
- Partner/customer onboarding, certification review, QA checks, and implementation guides.
- HR, Finance, Procurement, IT/helpdesk, Operations, Customer Success, Sales, and Marketing workflows.
- Agent evaluation, prompt libraries, eval libraries, failure-mode analysis, and safety testing.
