Most beginners use new AI models like a magic text box.

That is why the output feels random.

This video gives a better way to practice:

Pick projects where the AI has to plan, use tools, follow constraints, and keep working toward a finish line.

Here are the five project types from the video:

1. Clone a tool you understand
The example is a Whisper Flow-style local clone. This teaches privacy, product thinking, local models, and debugging.

2. Audit your AI workflow
Look at your previous Claude Code sessions and find what should become a reusable skill, automation, or fix.

3. Build an agentic OS
Create a simple dashboard for reports, repeated tasks, skills, and automations.

4. Review a real codebase
Ask for findings, severity, evidence, and fix priority.

5. Build from a PRD
Before a big project, write the requirements, constraints, and success criteria.

That is how you move from watching AI demos to actually building with AI.

Start with one project. Keep the scope small. Document every step.
