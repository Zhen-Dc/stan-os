Claude can be useful for digital marketing, but only if it is installed around real business work.

For example, a marketing team can use Claude to support:

Research.
Campaign briefs.
Content drafts.
Ad variations.
Reports.
Lead follow-up.
QA checks.

The mistake is treating Claude like a magic writing box.

The better move is to define one repeated marketing task and turn it into a workflow.

That is the skill companies actually care about:

Can you reduce the manual back-and-forth inside their marketing process?

Can you help the team move faster without losing quality?

Can you make the workflow repeatable?

That is where AI automation becomes practical.
