Do not install random things in Claude.

Install workflows.

For marketing teams, that means research, campaign briefs, content drafts, ad variations, reporting, follow-up, and QA.

The weak Claude setup:

A folder full of prompts.

The useful setup:

A marketing stack where every prompt, file, and automation moves a campaign from input to approved output.

Claude becomes useful for marketing when you give it a function.

Campaign strategist.
Research analyst.
Content planner.
Ad variation builder.
Reporting assistant.
Lead follow-up prep.

Most teams do not need more AI tools.

They need one repeated marketing workflow mapped clearly enough for AI to help without creating more chaos.

If Claude is helping your marketing team, it should know:

The buyer.
The offer.
The channel.
The proof.
The output format.
The review rule.

For app and SaaS teams, the opportunity is not "learn every AI trend."

The opportunity is turning repeated marketing tasks into workflows that save time every week.
