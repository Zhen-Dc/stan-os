1/ AI saves time when it becomes a workflow, not another tab. A role-based AI team beats a random one-off prompt.

2/ The prompt is not the asset. The repeatable process is. Manual content work gets expensive when every post starts from zero.

3/ Do not ask AI to guess your workflow. Give it the source, the rules, and the definition of done.

4/ The safest automation has a human review gate before anything reaches a client, customer, or audience.

5/ If a task happens every week, has clear inputs, and ends in a predictable output, it is a good AI workflow candidate.

6/ Split the work into research, draft, edit, visual plan, and QA. The win is not magic. It is repeatable operating leverage.
