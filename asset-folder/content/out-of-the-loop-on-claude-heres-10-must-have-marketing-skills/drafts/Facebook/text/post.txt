
If you run Meta or Google Ads, Sonnet 5 is useful for one practical reason:

It can turn account data into a ranked fix list.

That matters because most ad accounts do not need another generic marketing opinion.

They need decisions:

- scale this
- pause this
- test this
- rebuild this

The 10 Skills from the source split the work cleanly:

Meta Ads:

1. /tracking-audit
2. /kpi-map
3. /campaign-structure-audit
4. /adset-analysis
5. /creative-fatigue

Google Ads:

6. /wasted-spend-audit
7. /search-audit
8. /shopping-audit
9. /pmax-audit
10. /keyword-opportunities

The key is not asking Claude to "audit my ads."

The key is running one focused Skill against one leak type, then asking for the evidence, priority, verdict, and next action.

That is how Sonnet becomes an ad-account assistant instead of another chat window.
