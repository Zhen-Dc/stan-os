My mom asked me, "What the hell is Claude?" This image did the job, not me: Then she called. 6 more questions in 20 minutes. Her questions will teach you, better than I ever did: → "What do I type in the prompt box?" Copy this template and fill it with your context: "I want to \[TASK\] for \[RESULT\]. AskUserQuestion." Claude asks 3-5 questions. You tap the answers. Prompting, solved. She never asked about it again. → "You expect me to type all that?" No. Talk to your Claude. Download Wispr .ai (free). You speak 4x faster than you type. (She speaks 400x. Sorry mom.) → "Where did my file go?" Go to Claude desktop app. Locate "cowork." Open "Projects" and upload all the files there. She dropped in a messy PDF. She got an Excel. Saved on her computer. Found it herself. → "What the hell is a Claude skill?" Fair question. A long prompt turned into a Claude command that runs on its own, every single time. Building your own takes weeks. So I handed mine. Download my entire Skill library: [how-to-ai.guide](https://www.linkedin.com/redir/redirect?url=http%3A%2F%2Fhow-to-ai%2Eguide&urlhash=6cp_&trk=public_post-text)
. Open the email received in your inbox. Go to the given link and download the skills. Claude Customize → Skills → Upload. 2 minutes. Her Claude now works like mine. Yours can too. → "It's reading my emails??" Only if you let it. Gmail, calendar, meeting notes. Her first test: "prep me for tomorrow's meeting" She called back just to say "witchcraft." → "Why did nobody tell me this?" Because 98.75% of people have never tried Claude. Everyone's waiting to feel ready. She skipped the waiting. So can you. Save this for your own mom. Or dad, boss, friend. The one who keeps saying "I'm so behind." 10 minutes. 1 image. That's the whole entry fee. Get all 27 Claude Skills, free: [how-to-ai.guide](https://www.linkedin.com/redir/redirect?url=http%3A%2F%2Fhow-to-ai%2Eguide&urlhash=6cp_&trk=public_post-text)
