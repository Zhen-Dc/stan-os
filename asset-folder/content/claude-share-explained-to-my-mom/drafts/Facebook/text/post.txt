You do not need to understand every AI term before you use Claude.

Start with one task.

If someone is new, do not begin with "models", "tokens", "integrations", or "agents".

Give them this simple prompt:

"I want [task] for [result]. Ask me questions first, then create the plan."

Then show them the practical parts:

They can talk instead of typing everything.
They can upload a messy file into a Claude Project.
They can ask for an Excel table, checklist, summary, or plan.
They can save repeated instructions as Skills.
They can connect email or calendar only when they understand the permission.

That is the beginner path.

One task.
One result.
One small win.

That is how confidence starts.
