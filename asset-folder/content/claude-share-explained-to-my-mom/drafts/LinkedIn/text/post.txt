Most people do not need another explanation of Claude.

They need one useful result.

That is the whole onboarding problem.

When a non-technical person asks, "What is Claude?", do not start with models, tokens, projects, MCPs, skills, context windows, and all the things builders like to talk about.

Start here:

What do you want finished?

Then give them this first prompt:

"I want [task] for [result]. Ask me questions first, then create the plan."

That one line does a lot.

It removes the blank prompt problem.
It makes Claude collect context.
It turns a vague request into a real workflow.

After that, the rest becomes easier:

Use voice if typing slows them down.
Upload the messy file into a Claude Project.
Ask for the output they actually need.
Save repeated instructions as a skill.
Connect email or calendar only when they understand the permission.

This is how I would introduce Claude to a founder, teammate, parent, client, or creator who feels behind.

Do not sell the tool first.
Give them one small win.

The confidence comes after.

If you are trying to bring AI into your team, do not start with a feature tour.

Start with the task they already hate doing.
