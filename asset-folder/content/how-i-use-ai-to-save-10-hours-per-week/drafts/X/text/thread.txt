1/ AI saves time when it becomes a workflow, not another tab. The easiest way to start with AI is not advanced automation. It is one repeated task you already hate doing.

2/ The prompt is not the asset. The repeatable process is. Blank-page writing is a perfect AI workflow because the first draft is the bottleneck.

3/ Do not ask AI to guess your workflow. Give it the source, the rules, and the definition of done.

4/ The safest automation has a human review gate before anything reaches a client, customer, or audience.

5/ If a task happens every week, has clear inputs, and ends in a predictable output, it is a good AI workflow candidate.

6/ Use AI to create the first pass. Use your judgment to make it useful. The win is not magic. It is repeatable operating leverage.
