Stop treating AI agents like one-message assistants.

That is why the work keeps drifting.

If the task needs research, tools, files, checks, approvals, or multiple passes, a better prompt is not enough.

You need a loop.

A prompt says:
"Do this."

A loop says:
"Do this, check the result, decide what is next, and stop when the condition is met."

That is loop engineering.

The simple version:

Goal.
Context.
Action.
Feedback.
Stop condition.

Once those pieces are clear, AI becomes easier to trust because it is not running wild. It is working inside a defined process.
