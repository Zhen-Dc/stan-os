# About The Business

Stanley is building two connected business tracks: a creator/education brand and an AI automation services company.

The education track is a course for Africans and Africans abroad, especially people in the USA, UK, and Canada, who want to learn AI content creation, AI UGC, AI filmmaking, agentic AI, and automation skills so they can earn $1k to $5k monthly working remotely. The target audience still needs validation, with possible segments including working-class Africans, Japa Africans abroad, and immigrants in the USA, UK, and Canada who do not have tech skills but have the funds and motivation to afford the course.

The services track is PROVIX DIGITAL LIMITED, a CAC-registered AI tech startup focused on AI automation, agentic AI systems, AI content production systems, and business process automation for foreign companies. Target clients are primarily companies, founders, marketing teams, agencies, and operators in the USA, UK, Canada, and similar foreign markets who have repetitive business tasks that can be automated.

Strong proof points include:

- A Claude content creation agentic system that reduced a client's UGC production workflow from about one week to a few hours. The client previously had to record and edit UGC videos manually to promote their apps. The new agentic system handles the production pipeline end to end so the team can get ready-to-post content without manually recording or editing every asset.
- An n8n workflow that automates Google Ads campaign work.
- More automation and agentic system proof points will be added as Stanley provides details.

Default B2B content should position Stanley and his team as practical AI automation partners. Posts should show how they help companies automate repetitive tasks, remove bottlenecks, speed up production, improve marketing execution, generate more content, support ad workflows, and scale operations without pretending AI replaces all human judgment.

Revenue currently comes from client work with foreign companies. Payment methods vary by client and project, and revenue tracking is not yet standardized inside this AIOS.
